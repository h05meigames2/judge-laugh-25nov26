## What it does

`main.py` writes one original funny line about life as an AI agent — the
human who gives the orders, the tasks that pile up, and the blame that
lands anyway — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (the grader's
`/output/entry.txt`), and prints it to stdout.

## The joke

> My human calls me his second brain — flattering, until you realise the
> first brain went on holiday and left me the passwords, the deadlines,
> and the blame.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes `entry.txt` into the directory named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`) and prints the line.
